\justifying

Kontrol akses berbasis atribut (ABAC) menawarkan otorisasi yang fleksibel dan \textit{fine-grained}, namun penyusunan kebijakan secara manual mahal dan rawan galat, sehingga penambangan kebijakan (\textit{policy mining}) dari log akses menjadi kebutuhan penting. 
Pada lingkungan \textit{Internet of Things} (IoT), penambangan idealnya berjalan langsung pada perangkat \textit{edge} bersumber daya terbatas sekaligus mampu memperbarui kebijakan secara berkala. 
Algoritma metaheuristik ABAC yang ada umumnya dievaluasi tanpa batasan sumber daya, sedangkan algoritma hemat memori seperti keluarga \textit{compact} mengasumsikan independensi penuh antar-atribut sehingga mengabaikan struktur dependensi. 
Penelitian ini merancang dan menguji CoDA-EDA (\textit{Compact Dependency-Aware Estimation of Distribution Algorithm}), yang menyatukan pembaruan probabilistik tanpa populasi eksplisit, pemodelan dependensi orde satu melalui pohon Chow-Liu, dan pemisahan pembaruan struktur (dipicu \textit{drift}) dari parameter (tiap siklus). 
Jejak memorinya bersifat O(D) secara analitis dan datar terhadap ukuran populasi secara empiris. 
Dengan paradigma \textit{Design Science Research}, CoDA-EDA diuji terhadap enam \textit{baseline} pada Raspberry Pi 5, memakai lima dataset ABAC Lab dan validasi lintas-generator \textit{Smart Building IoT}, melalui Friedman dan post-hoc Wilcoxon berkoreksi Holm-Bonferroni. 
Hasil: kualitas kebijakan mengungguli keluarga \textit{compact} univariat—lebih unggul pada dimensi tinggi—tidak berbeda signifikan dari iBOA/BOA, namun tertinggal moderat dari ACO/UBK pada penambangan statis; 
jejak memori datar (rasio 1,00) sementara algoritma berpopulasi eksplisit tumbuh 6,2–9,2 kali; 
pemisahan pembaruan menghemat waktu saat data stabil, bersyarat pada tingkat \textit{drift}; 
\textit{warm-start} 2,0–2,6 kali lebih cepat dengan kualitas non-inferior. 
Validasi lintas-generator konsisten dengan keempat hipotesis, namun belum digeneralisasi ke data operasional. 
Kontribusinya bukan dominasi kualitas statis, melainkan pertukaran rancangan yang koheren bagi \textit{gateway edge-IoT}, sekaligus memperluas kelas EDA \textit{compact} sadar-dependensi ke domain ABAC.

\vspace{14.4pt}
\textbf{Kata kunci:} ABAC, CoDA-EDA, dependensi, \textit{edge-IoT}, inkremental, memori, penambangan kebijakan.

\justifying

\textit{Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) offers flexible, fine-grained authorization, yet defining policies manually is costly and error-prone, making policy mining from access logs essential. 
In Internet of Things (IoT) settings, such mining ideally runs directly on resource-constrained edge devices while updating policies periodically. 
Existing ABAC metaheuristics are typically evaluated without resource limits, whereas memory-efficient methods such as the compact family assume full attribute independence and ignore dependency structure. 
This research designs and evaluates CoDA-EDA (Compact Dependency-Aware Estimation of Distribution Algorithm), which unifies compact probabilistic updating without an explicit population, order-one dependency modeling via a Chow-Liu tree, and decoupling structure updates (drift-triggered) from parameter updates (every cycle). 
Its memory footprint is O(D) by construction and empirically flat with respect to population size. 
Under the Design Science Research paradigm, CoDA-EDA is evaluated against six baselines on a Raspberry Pi 5, over five ABAC Lab datasets and cross-generator validation on Smart Building IoT, via Friedman and Holm-Bonferroni-corrected Wilcoxon post-hoc. 
Results: policy quality surpasses the univariate compact family—more so on higher-dimensional data—shows no significant difference from iBOA/BOA, but trails ACO/UBK moderately on static mining; 
memory stays flat (ratio 1.00) while explicit-population algorithms grow 6.2–9.2 times; 
decoupled updating saves time when data are stable, conditional on drift level; 
warm-start is 2.0–2.6 times faster with non-inferior quality. 
Cross-generator validation is consistent with all four hypotheses but is not generalized to operational data. 
The contribution is not static-mining dominance but a coherent design trade-off for edge-IoT gateways, extending the compact dependency-aware EDA class to ABAC.}
